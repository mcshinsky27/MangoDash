WURLITZER ZODIAC
The Legend of American Jukeboxes

Musical Innovation

The Wurlitzer Zodiac represents one of the most iconic designs in jukebox history. Introduced in the late 1950s, the Zodiac model brought a new level of sophistication and visual appeal to the American jukebox scene.

Known for its distinctive starburst pattern and vibrant color options, the Zodiac was more than just a music player—it was a work of art that transformed any space into a nostalgic entertainment venue.

1959
The Zodiac Arrives
Wurlitzer introduces the Model 1300 Zodiac, featuring revolutionary starburst lighting patterns and advanced selection technology.
1960s
Golden Era
The Zodiac becomes a symbol of American pop culture, appearing in diners, arcades, and entertainment venues across the nation.
1970s
Production Ends
As the jukebox industry evolves, the Zodiac ceases production, becoming a highly sought-after collector's item.
Today
Living Legacy
Restored Wurlitzer Zodiac jukeboxes continue to bring joy and nostalgia, preserving the golden age of American music entertainment.
